# Semrush top 100 most searched people (seed)

Source: Semrush "Most Popular People", https://www.semrush.com/popular/people/, ranks 1 to 100 of 456, page dated March 2026, read on 25 November 2026. Ranking is Google monthly global search volume for the person's name.

`group` and `in_seed` are our own annotations, made from memory and not verified:

- `deceased`: historical or dead figures. Excluded from the seed (`in_seed` = no).
- `politician` and `royal`: kept in the seed on purpose, flagged so they can be dropped from a published list.
- `ambiguous term`: the search term may partly mean something else ("pink").

Volumes are banded, so many people share one number and rank order inside a band is arbitrary.
