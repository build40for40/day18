These 20 people are **not from Semrush**. They were picked by hand (Claude's guess at who is globally popular and missing from the Semrush list, which has no living people beyond rank 305 and under-represents creators and non-English markets). None appear in Semrush ranks 1 to 456 as far as we saw. Treat them as a separate, weaker seed.

Run on 25 November 2026 with the same method as `guardian-crossover-scores.md`: Guardian keyword tag, 12 months (25 Nov 2025 to 25 Nov 2026), `other_sections` = sections other than the home section with at least 1 article, opinion ignored, football merged into sport.

**How the 10 were chosen:** `guess_order` is the guessed popularity order, set before looking at any Guardian data. The 10 chosen are the first 10 in that order that have a Guardian tag and at least one usable article. They were not picked by score, but skipping people with no data means the 10 are not a fair sample of the 20.
