# Semrush living seed: 100 people

Source: Semrush "Most Popular People", https://www.semrush.com/popular/people/, page dated March 2026, read on 25 November 2026. The ranking is Google monthly global search volume for the person's name. `rank` is the person's rank on that page (out of 456).

The CSV holds Semrush ranks 1 to 155. Every row marked `in_seed` = yes is a living person, and there are exactly 100 of them: the 82 living people in ranks 1 to 100, plus the next 18 living people (ranks 101 to 155). Deceased and historical figures are listed for transparency but are out of the seed (`in_seed` = no).

`group` and `in_seed` are our own annotations, made from memory and not verified, and someone listed as living may have changed since:

- `deceased`: historical or dead figures, excluded.
- `politician` and `royal`: kept in the seed on purpose, flagged so they can be dropped from a published list.
- `ambiguous term`: the search term may partly mean something else ("pink").

Volumes are banded, so many people share one number and rank order inside a band is arbitrary. Ranks 156 to 456 were not added: a hand read of ranks up to 400 found only a handful more living people, such as Virat Kohli (181), Shah Rukh Khan (193) and Usain Bolt (195).
